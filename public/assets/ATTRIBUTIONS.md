**Historical integrity policy:** real sites (Cổ Loa, Trà Kiệu, Óc Eo, Má Thẹn, Hòa Bình) are labeled
“Real site — fictionalized expedition model”. Liên Lậu Survey Grid and Đắk Sơn Basin are explicitly
fictional. In-game artifact attributions are rule-based simulations, never documented discoveries.
